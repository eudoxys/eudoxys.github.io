_Eudoxys Sciences releases `buildings` package to access ORNL inventory of US buildings_.

The `buildings` package provides access to an inventory of US buildings generated by ORNL in 2023. 

For more information, see the [Eudoxys `buildings` package on github](https://github.com/eudoxys/buildings).
